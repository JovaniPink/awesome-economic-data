The root LICENSE at each listed PR head was inspected and contained MIT. No separate
CLA, copyright assignment, or conflicting license for the submitted catalog prose was
found in those inspected records. Publisher licenses discussed in submissions concern
the linked datasets/services, not ownership of our descriptions. This is a public-record
review, not independent verification of every submitter's employment or chain of title.

MIT permits redistribution, modification, and sublicensing subject to retaining its
notice. The transition preserves that notice verbatim in LICENSE-CODE and NOTICE.md;
earlier contributors' grants remain available, and the new CC BY policy is not presented
as retroactive consent. GitHub's contribution-under-repository-license rule is supporting
context, not proof of copyright assignment. No contributor was contacted for new consent.

## Implementation and boundaries

- CC BY 4.0 covers original content and compilation under LICENSING.md; MIT covers code.
- Earlier MIT material retains its existing terms and notice; third-party attribution is preserved.
- Code of conduct remains unchanged, with its existing Contributor Covenant version attribution and separately documented historical CC BY 4.0 source license.
- Linked datasets, websites, APIs, logos, quoted material, and trademarks retain their own terms.
- No transfer of ownership to The Measured Studios is asserted.
- Resource identities, creation dates, source license fields, and catalog-wide review dates are unchanged.
- The license issue remains open for owner review. This change does not claim upstream Awesome admission: that project has additional editorial and AI-generation rules beyond licensing.
